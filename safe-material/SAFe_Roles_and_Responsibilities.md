# SAFe Roles and Responsibilities

Extracted from source material in this folder.

---

## Portfolio Level

| Role | Main Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Enterprise Executives** | Connect portfolio to enterprise strategy, maintain Portfolio Vision and investment funding |
| **Business Owners** | Ensure ART priorities align with portfolio; engage in PI Planning and Inspect & Adapt |
| **Enterprise Architect** | Long-term technology evolution, NFRs, align technology standards across the portfolio |
| **Value Management Office (VMO)** | Facilitate portfolio sync, coordinate governance, develop ART execution patterns |
| **Lean-Agile Center of Excellence (LACE)** | Small team dedicated to implementing SAFe; coaches, trains, and facilitates workshops |
| **Epic Owner** | Define Epics and Lean business cases; coordinate through Portfolio Kanban; facilitate implementation |
| **Solution Portfolio Management** | Guide a set of integrated Solutions across a portfolio, ensuring alignment across multiple Solution offerings |
| **SAFe Program Consultant (SPC)** | Lead ART identification workshops; coach and guide SAFe implementation across the organisation |

---

## Large Solution (Solution Train) Level

| Role | Main Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Solution Train Engineer (STE)** | Support decentralized Solution Train execution; coordinate operational excellence at the Large Solution level |
| **Solution Management** | Prioritize Capabilities for the Solution Train Backlog; split Epics with Epic Owners |
| **Solution Architect** | Coordinate architectural concerns across the Solution Train |
| **Epic Owner** | Coordinates Epics at the Large Solution level; works with Solution Management and Architects |
| **Enterprise Architect** | Aligns technology standards and Roadmaps across Solution Trains and the portfolio |
| **Suppliers** | External partners developing Solutions under Agile contracting practices |

---

## ART (Agile Release Train) Level

| Role | Main Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Release Train Engineer (RTE)** | Support decentralized ART execution; coordinate operational excellence across the Agile Release Train |
| **Product Management** | Prioritize business Features for the ART Backlog; split Epics with Epic Owners |
| **System Architect** | Split Enabler Epics, prioritize Enabler Features, align with Enterprise Architects on technology standards |
| **Business Owners** | Assign business value to Team PI Objectives; actively engage in PI Planning and reviewing ART plans |
| **Epic Owner** | Facilitates Epic implementation at the ART level; works with Product Management and Agile Teams |
| **System Team** | Integrate work across Value Streams; support releases and effective DevSecOps capabilities |

---

## Team Level

| Role | Main Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Scrum Master / Team Coach (SM/TC)** | Support team-level Agile execution; share and develop practices via Communities of Practice |
| **Agile Teams** | Build and test MVPs; implement Features and Enablers as directed by Product Management |
| **Customer** | End recipient of Solutions; provides feedback that drives hypothesis validation and value delivery decisions |
